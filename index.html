About

I'm a student at the Arts et Métiers campus of Rabat, working toward an engineering degree with a focus on mechatronics and robotics.

What drew me to this field is the mix: a robotics problem is never just mechanical, or just electrical, or just code — it's usually all three at once, and figuring out how they fit together is most of the interesting work. I'm early in that process, currently building up the fundamentals in control systems, embedded electronics, and mechanical design.

Based in Rabat, Morocco.
